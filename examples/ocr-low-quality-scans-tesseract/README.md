The `IronTesseract` OCR Class offers C# and .NET developers precise control to integrate OCR (image and PDF to text conversion) capabilities into their software, while also allowing for customization to optimize performance according to specific needs.

Developers can fine-tune the balance between speed and accuracy by adjusting various settings based on real-world examples. These settings include: `CleanBackgroundNoise`, `EnhanceContrast`, `EnhanceResolution`, `Language`, `Strategy`, `RotateAndStraighten`, `ColorSpace`, `DetectWhiteTextOnDarkBackgrounds`, and `InputImageType`.
